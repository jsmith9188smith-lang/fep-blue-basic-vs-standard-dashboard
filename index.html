Pros & Cons Summary

Basic Advantages

  • Significantly lower premiums
  • No deductible
  • Predictable flat copays for many outpatient services
  • Solid diabetes program access

Basic Drawbacks

  • Preferred-provider only
  • High inpatient costs for multi-day stays
  • Higher DME coinsurance (35%)
  • Higher overall OOP maximum

Standard Advantages

  • Much lower inpatient cost ($350/admission)
  • Lower DME coinsurance (15%)
  • Lower OOP maximum
  • Out-of-network flexibility + largest formulary
  • Better Mail Service / Specialty pharmacy options

Standard Drawbacks

  • Higher premiums
  • Deductible applies to many services
  • Slightly higher specialist office copays in some cases

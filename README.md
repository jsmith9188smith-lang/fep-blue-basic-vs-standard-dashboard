# FEP Blue Basic vs Standard – 2026 Dashboard

**Type 1 Diabetes Comparison for a TSA/TSO Employee**

This interactive dashboard compares the 2026 **FEP Blue Cross Blue Shield Basic** and **Standard** health plans, focused on the needs of a Type 1 diabetic using:

- Novolog insulin  
- Tandem t:slim X2 insulin pump  
- Dexcom G7 CGM  
- Supplies from Edgepark  
- Endocrinology care at UAB/Kirklin Clinic  
- History of extended (up to 7-day) hospital stays

## Key Findings (Sample Year)

In a year that includes **one 7-day hospital admission** + typical pump/CGM supplies + insulin:

| Plan     | Est. Total Cost (Premium + OOP) |
|----------|---------------------------------|
| Basic    | ~$9,500 – $9,700                |
| Standard | ~$6,900 – $7,100                |

**Standard is projected to cost ~$2,500–$2,800 less overall** for this profile, driven by the much lower inpatient copay ($350 vs up to $2,975) and lower DME coinsurance (15% vs 35%).

## Disclaimer

All figures are **illustrative estimates** based on official 2026 FEP Blue materials and publicly available data.  
Actual costs depend on Plan allowance, prior authorizations, quantity limits, formulary status, and claims adjudication.  

**This is not official advice.** Always verify with:
- FEP Customer Care: 1-800-411-BLUE  
- The official 2026 Service Benefit Plan brochure (RI 71-005)  
- Your providers and Edgepark
